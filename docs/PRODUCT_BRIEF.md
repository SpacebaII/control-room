# Product brief

## Problem

Operational programs are rarely controlled in one place. Plans live in a task tool, costs in spreadsheets, decisions in meeting notes, risks in a register, and sponsor updates in slide decks. Managers spend time reconciling the story before they can manage the work.

## Product thesis

Control Room treats schedule, cost, risk, capacity, decisions, communication, reporting, and evidence as one control system. Health is calculated from records and always explains itself. Assistance proposes work but never bypasses accountable approval.

## Primary user

An operations program director coordinating multiple interdependent workstreams with executive governance, lead ownership, financial controls, and a recurring reporting cadence.

## Demonstration scenario

Horizon Service Group is opening a Denver service hub while introducing a field scheduling platform, preparing vendors, and training regional leaders. The seeded portfolio includes ordinary delivery friction: a failed vendor patch, a delayed carrier circuit, forecast pressure, overloaded specialists, incomplete evidence, and pending sponsor decisions.

## Success criteria

- A visitor understands the program position and supporting evidence within two minutes.
- A manager can move from an exception to a controlled decision and audited outcome.
- Mobile users can find evidence, comment, and approve without navigating dense desktop controls.
- Public demonstrations make no model calls.
- Owner assistance remains bounded, cited, reviewable, and non-mutating until accepted.
